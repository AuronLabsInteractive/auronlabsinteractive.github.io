Account & data deletion

You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways:

1

In the game (immediate)

Open the profile menu, scroll to Danger zone, and tap Delete account. You will be asked to confirm twice — the second step requires typing your email address. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

2

By email (no app needed)

Write to auronlabs.studio@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request, from the email address registered in the game. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • your registration (email address and login credentials)
  • all of your player profiles
  • every save, statistic, achievement and running game
  • all countries you built in the country builder
  • your Thaler balance and every unlock

Nothing is retained after deletion except what we are legally required to keep. Guest-mode players have no server-side data at all — their game lives only on their own device.

2. APPLICABILITY & AGE RESTRICTION

The Game is rated PEGI 12 / ESRB Teen. It is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EU jurisdictions without parental consent). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under these ages. We strongly advise users to choose a pseudonymous username and refrain from using their real names.

3. DATA WE COLLECT AND HOW WE USE IT

We process data under GDPR principles (lawful basis: contract performance Art. 6(1)(b) and legitimate interest Art. 6(1)(f)).

A) ACCOUNT & CLOUD DATA (Supabase EU)

To enable cloud saves, cross-device synchronization, secure currency (Tallér) balances, and to prevent memory manipulation, we collect:

Account credentials: email address and securely salted/hashed password tokens (via Supabase Authentication).

Gameplay state: user-selected pseudonymous username, country profiles, game progress, achievements, and unlocked assets.

Guest mode: if you choose Guest Mode, gameplay is tied to a pseudonymous, device-level token without collecting an email address.

B) TECHNICAL & CRASH LOGS

In the event of an application error, a local crash log is generated (error stack trace, app version, user-agent/device model). When synced for diagnostic purposes, IP addresses and device parameters are logged in secure server logs to maintain platform stability.

C) THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free, we integrate Google AdMob. AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as Device Advertising ID) and IP addresses to serve non-intrusive ads.

Content filtering: all ads are hard-restricted to Teen/PG levels. Sensitive categories (gambling, adult, alcohol) are strictly blocked.

EU consent (GDPR): EEA users are presented with a User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent banner upon first launch to allow or opt out of personalized tracking.

4. DATA STORAGE AND EU LOCATION

All cloud database infrastructure (Supabase) is hosted within the European Economic Area (EEA / EU Region). Your data does not leave the EU/EEA without appropriate GDPR-compliant safeguards.

5. USER RIGHTS & ACCOUNT DELETION

Under GDPR, you hold the following rights:

Right to access & export: you may request a copy of your server-stored profile data.

Right to erasure: you can permanently purge your account, saves and email from our database directly in the Game via Settings > Danger zone > Delete account.

Web-based erasure: if you do not have the app installed, you may request full data deletion by emailing auronlabs.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

Fiók és adatok törlése

Bármikor kérheted a fiókod és minden hozzá tartozó adatod teljes törlését. Két út áll rendelkezésre:

1

A játékban (azonnali)

Nyisd meg a profil-menüt, görgess a Veszélyes zóna részhez, és koppints a Fiók törlése gombra. Két lépésben kell megerősítened — a másodiknál be kell gépelned az e-mail címedet. A törlés azonnali és visszavonhatatlan.

2

E-mailben (az app nélkül is)

Írj a auronlabs.studio@gmail.com címre Account Deletion Request tárggyal, a játékban regisztrált e-mail címedről. A kérelmeket 30 napon belül teljesítjük.

Mi törlődik

  • a regisztrációd (e-mail cím és belépési adatok)
  • az összes játékos-profilod
  • minden mentés, statisztika, eredmény és futó játék
  • az országépítőben létrehozott összes országod
  • a Tallér-egyenleged és minden feloldásod

A törlés után semmi nem marad meg, kivéve amit jogszabály kötelezővé tesz. A vendég módban játszóknak egyáltalán nincs szerveroldali adatuk — a játékuk kizárólag a saját készülékükön él.
